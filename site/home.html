Two ways to practise the same questions

Both formats share the question bank, filters, definitions and source library. Each keeps its own progress, and you can switch between them on any question.

Multiple choice

Best for a quick check of what you know, or a first pass through a topic.

  1. Choose one of four answers. Keys 1 to 4 work too.
  2. See the correct answer, the model answer and the Google excerpt.
  3. Missed questions go to your review queue. Your score counts first attempts only.
Start multiple choice

Answer aloud

Best for rehearsing the way you will actually answer in an interview.

  1. Read the question and answer out loud. An optional 60-second timer keeps you honest.
  2. Reveal the model answer and compare it with the Google excerpt.
  3. Mark it “review later” or “confident”. Your review queue collects the ones to repeat.
